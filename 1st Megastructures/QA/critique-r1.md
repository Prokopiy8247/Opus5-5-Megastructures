# Critique r1: Abyss, run f02

- **Run id:** f02 (captures `QA/captures/f02_keys`, `QA/captures/f02_beats`; metrics `QA/metrics/f02_keys`, `QA/metrics/f02_beats`)
- **Date:** 2026-10-30
- **Panel:** critic panel round 1 (Part 11.5) with five read-only critics: A scale and awe, B technical artefacts, C art-direction fidelity, D pacing and reveal, E spec gaps.
- **Merge rules:** Duplicates are merged into one row. Each row keeps the strongest severity among its sources and all of their evidence. Bracketed IDs in the Description column name the source findings. Where critics proposed different fix values, the Fix column records the range or says which proposal wins. Metric FAILs that another critic showed to be tool artefacts are listed separately and are not counted as picture defects.
- **Severity:** S1 blocker, S2 major, S3 minor (Part 11.4).

## Critics' verdicts

- **A, scale and awe:** Of the five money shots, only KS6 (the fan seen from the gantry) reads as colossal. KS7b, KS2b, KS5b and KS2a each fail on scale for a concrete, fixable reason: column stubs with lit holes in the honeycomb; a cluttered floor of city blocks under the bridge; the deck hiding half of the fan; shaft walls with no readable rhythm. KS5a is readable but flat. The compress-then-release structure works, but the release frames lack depth. Look-dev lifted the dark end of the image (KS7b p1 rose from 0.07 to 0.115 between f01 and f02), and the distant layers sit too close in value: in KS2a, C3 and the far wall differ by about 0.01 luma.
- **B, technical artefacts:** There are no magenta, NaN or firefly defects, and the horizons and fog-to-sky blends are clean. All 9 banding FAILs are metric artefacts. One money shot fails outright: KS2b has the capsule-shadow blob and side strips lit by the city. KS7b is marred by dotted, aliased neighbour bridges and visible tier-dither stipple. Down-facing surfaces crush to black across the film, the lift-deck bevel highlights render as dashes, the beat-3 lamp globes are flat, and a beam/depth leak shows when looking back along the gantry. All of these must be fixed before the zero-defect bar is met.
- **C, art-direction fidelity:** The cold, foggy machine mood is mostly consistent across beats 2-6, and KS5a, KS5b and KS6 come close to the BLAME!/Control target. The materials are not credible: board-formed concrete, steel wear, grating and floors read as smooth plaster, stamped decals and graybox planes. Amber spills far beyond "rare human presence", and shadows crush to pure black. Look problems break two of the three money shots: KS2b (brown capsule shadow, a cluttered and over-bright void) and KS7b (a tabletop maze of column stubs instead of an abyssal honeycomb).
- **D, pacing and reveal:** The sequence has the right shape: the honeycomb comes last, the fan (KS6) and the city (KS5a) land, and the outdoor brightness gives a real release. Several staged reveals fail as they actually play. The doorway promise turns into a dimming. The vertigo shot looks down onto a floor of city blocks. The lift's look down the shaft shows only the deck for about 9 s. In the finale the door is shown before the reveal, the lamp ignition happens off-screen, and the honeycomb reads at car-park scale with no darks.
- **E, spec gaps:** Against its own 14.5/14.10 lines, f02 does well on KS2a, KS5a (more than 1000 windows, cells about 13 x 15.5 px), the KS6 composition and the KS7b counts (about 240-350 amber dots, horizon 34-37 % from the top). Several specified frames show something else: KS2b looks down onto a lit city, KS5c and the -55 ride cue show mostly deck, the deck hides the lower half of the KS5b fan, KS1 has no far doorway layer, and the KS3 pour lifts do not read. Most of these slipped through because the lint is blind to occlusion and the 14.5 image checks are not implemented. Several arbitrations that 1.6.2 requires exist only in code comments, not in DEVELOPMENT.md.
