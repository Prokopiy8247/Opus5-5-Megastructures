## Critics' verdicts

- **A, scale and awe:** f03 is a clear step up. The r1 blockers that broke scale by accident are fixed: the city floor under KS2b, the half fan in KS5b, the peg stubs in the honeycomb, the black crush and the over-bright hull. KS6 is sublime, and KS5a and KS5b now read as colossal. The height money shots still do not sell height. KS2b is a plank over flat black. In KS7b, KS7c and B7 the honeycomb reads as a hex-tiled floor just under the pier, because the near cells out-contrast the foreground and there is no air between balcony and terrace. KS5c is still half deck. The remaining wins are depth staging (terrace haze, lit ring funnels, backlit far layers, a contre-jour fan), not new geometry.
- **B, technical artefacts:** Most r1 technical defects are fixed. There are no magenta, NaN, firefly, banding or tier-stipple defects, the pure-black crush is gone (near-black 0 % in 98 of 99 frames), and KS2b, KS7b and KS7d no longer show the lit city, capsule blob, stubs, dotted bridges or black corbels. New technical blockers: a 1-px pure-black ring at the tunnel mouth in B6_start, hard-edged beam and shadow-prism silhouettes, and hot 1-px specular hairlines in the beat-6 look-back and on the KS7d blades. R1-05 and R1-08 (S1) and R1-11, R1-24, R1-27 and R1-28 (S2) are still open. Of the 11 metric FAILs, only B3_middle (+0.005) and KS7b readable_cells reflect the picture, and neither is a visible artefact.
- **C, art-direction fidelity:** A clear step up from f02. The palette is disciplined: no pure black, amber in small local pools, and the cold fog mood holds across all seven beats. KS6, B6_start and the new KS2b read as intended. Two problems still keep the film from looking like the work of a senior team. Materials: the gallery walls, C1, the shaft walls and the floors show no visible board-formed concrete or wear, and the climax honeycomb reads as a clean moulded tray. Human-presence amber: distant lamps and lamp cores render cream-white instead of amber.
- **D, pacing and reveal:** The film now has a working emotional arc. Gallery, bridge, ledge, hall, lift, fan, tunnel and vista compress and release in order, and most r1 staging failures are fixed. Three reveals still fail as they play: the ending never shows the door opening or its amber light; the honeycomb reads as a hex grating just below the pier; the lift's 6 s look "down into the shaft" is still about half deck. Several staged cues are too faint or too short to register on YouTube: the ignition (about 1.8 s), the vertigo hold's black side strips, the hall exit onto a blank wall, and the falling bolt (visible about 1.3 s).
- **E, spec gaps:** f03 closes most r1 gaps. The KS2b strips, the KS5b fan, the KS7b honeycomb, the near-black floor, the ignition and the door timing now meet their 14.5 and 14.10 lines, and the occlusion-aware lint and image checks run. Three S1 rows remain: KS5c still shows half deck, the doorway reveal is still veiled by a sourceless haze card, and the KS3 pour lifts are not readable. Two new S2 gaps sit on climax material: KS7b has 125 readable cells against the 150 required at 1080p, and the ending's amber sliver is missing.
