## Architecture
- **Assemblies**: `Megastructure.Runtime` (Scripts/Runtime + Beats via `Megastructure.Beats.asmref`), `Megastructure.Editor`, `Megastructure.Tests.EditMode`, `Megastructure.Tests.PlayMode`.
- **Single sources of truth**: `ThreadConstants` (every number of Part 14), `ThreadPalette` (every colour; the generated `Shaders/Generated/ThreadPalette.hlsl` exposes `PAL_*` macros), `ThreadMath` (path, corridor, FOV, thread law, ascent curves, per-beat blends; pure double C#), `FogMath` (C# mirror of the HLSL sky/fog for tests).
- **Shader contract**: `Shaders/Include/ThreadCommon.hlsl` declares all global uniforms (`_Th*`, mirrored in `ShaderIds.cs`), `ThreadSkyColor`, the shared fog `ThreadFogApply` (distance + bank + deck), the analytic cable shadow and the flat-with-a-rim recipe `ThreadShadeFlat`.
- **Boot**: `GameBootstrap` discovers every `IBeatBuilder` by reflection (ordered by Name) and builds the world at load behind black; `ProgressDirector` (execution order 500, after the camera) computes all uniforms in double each frame, sinks the ground group by `-A_virt`, drives the sun and `GradeState`, calls the `IWorldUpdater`s. Systems talk through `ThreadServices` interfaces (player, camera, audio, fade).
- **Frames**: near frame in true world coordinates (< 600 m from origin); the sky (gradient, far thread, sun, stars, planet, Earth, cloud sea, shadow band) is analytic in the skybox shader.

## Decisions (ADR log)
- **D-001 Runtime generation.** Heavy meshes are generated at load (behind the black fade) by C# builders; scenes, materials, palette and settings are generated by editor scripts and committed. Reason: Part 7.4 (load behind fade) and small repository; the same generators are called by EditMode tests.
- **D-002 Builder discovery by reflection.** Decouples compile-time dependencies between parallel builders (Part 5.3). Mono backend (no stripping issue).
- **D-003 Sky rays get no distance fog.** The closed-form height-fog integral to infinity is `tau = k H_f e^{-y/H_f} / r_y`; with beat 1 values (k = 3.912/1600, H_f = 900) the zenith would get tau = 2.2 (89 % horizon colour) and the gradient of 14.2.1 would be washed out. The sky gradient already is the colour of the atmosphere toward infinity (it equals the fog colour at the horizon), so sky rays receive only the finite bank and deck terms (`ThreadFogApplySky`). Geometry still receives the full fog, and the ground at 14 km is fully fogged to the horizon colour, so the horizon stays seamless.
- **D-004 Cable shadow sign.** 14.7.7 writes `cableShadow = t > 0 ? 1 - smoothstep(42, 58, n) : 0`, which is the shadow *amount*; the tests of 14.7.7/14.10 (0 at s = 664 in shade, 1 at s = 600 in sun) and its use as a `litTotal` factor require the lit factor `t > 0 ? smoothstep(42, 58, n) : 1`. Implemented as the lit factor, with the `t > 0` test softened over +-2 m (an arm lying exactly on t = 0 would otherwise show a discontinuity; such points with n < 42 are inside the cable).
- **D-005 Analytic cable shadow from s = 262.** The cable does not cast shadow maps (a 160 m shadow distance would end the cable's shadow abruptly on the terrace). The analytic cylinder shadow is enabled from the near-cable activation (s = 262, inside the bank) instead of only in beats 5-7, so the terrace also receives it; values on the deck are unchanged.
- **D-006 Deck slab gate.** The deck slab is placed relative to the camera (`top = y_cam - (A - 3000)`); evaluated literally, every upward ray from below would cross 1 200 m of 0.09 /m density from s = 400 on (white sky in beat 5). The term is therefore gated by `smoothstep(1.2 km, 1.8 km, A)` (s ~ 569 to 578) (0 at s = 560, 0.9995 at s = 578, 1 from s = 578.2) so the white-out begins as the camera approaches the deck; a 100 m bottom skin and the specified 150 m top skin keep every optical-depth gradient smooth. The optical depth is integrated in closed form (exact antiderivative of the smoothstep profile), not sampled.
- **D-007 Ground group off at s = 590, not 597.** Once the camera is above the deck top (s > 595.4), a ray to the sunk ground crosses the whole slab and the ground is drawn as flat CLOUD_TOP, while the analytic sea behind it shows the patterned cloud field; deactivating at 597 would pop the lower hemisphere from flat to patterned. At s = 590 (A = 2.597 km) the camera is inside the slab (optical depth about 30 up, 67 down, far above the threshold of 5 of 14.6), so the switch is invisible; re-activation at s < 586 (hysteresis, also inside the slab). The bank term switches with the ground group.
- **D-008 Sky gradient interpolation.** "Smooth interpolation" between the H, M, Z stops is a C1 cubic Hermite in `t = sqrt(dirY)` with zero tangents at H and Z and a Catmull-Rom tangent at M, clamped to the stop range. Plain smoothstep per segment has zero slope at the 25-degree stop and creates a visible flat band.
- **D-009 Shade colours.** The generic shade is `albedo * (0.52, 0.58, 0.78)`; materials whose spec names a shade token use it explicitly (cable TEAL_DEEP, right-row colossi CORAL_DEEP).

- **D-010 Helix radius = 440 / (2 pi) exactly** (70.0281750 m, the printed 70.028 is its rounding). With 70.028 one turn is 439.9989 m and PathPoint jumps 1.1 mm at s = 840 (T-PATH requires < 1 mm). The cable axis x and the helix centre use the same exact value (within T-SCALE-4's +-0.02 m).
- **D-011 Bank Y transitions 8 m** (half-edge 4 m instead of 2 m). With 4 m transitions T-BANK failed on 4 of 2000 rays (worst 2.46x the tolerance, transmission error 0.0137); 14.7.6 names exactly this remedy. With 8 m: 0 failures, worst 0.56x.
- **D-012 Limiter ceiling 0.84** (-1.51 dBFS). 14.3.1 writes `0.89 tanh(x/0.89)` "(ceiling about -1.5 dBFS)", but 0.89 is -1.01 dBFS; 14.12 and T-AUDIO require -1.5 dBFS, so the number follows the dB requirement (Part 1.6 arbitration).

- **D-013 Distance-fog colour along the ray.** `ThreadFogMix` colours the distance term with `ThreadSkyColor(dir.y, D)` of the ray being fogged (horizon colour at and below the horizon, so the horizon rule of 14.7.2 holds), not always the horizon colour; otherwise the cable top at y = 600 in beat 4 (tau about 0.54) fades to warm cream against a blue zenith. Implemented without changing signatures (`ThreadFogTaus` records the ray's y in a per-invocation static).
- **D-014 Tone curve (ThreadGrade).** Khronos PBR Neutral without its 0.04 toe offset (the toe exists for PBR F0 specular, which Thread has none of; with it INK 0.0123 would become 0.0009 and the night zenith 0.0012 would vanish). Identity below the knee (0.76), C1, monotonic, hue-preserving. Contrast is `f(s) = s + k (s - 0.5) 16 s^2 (1 - s)^2` in sRGB code space (fixes 0, 0.5, 1; monotonic; URP's LogC contrast at +8 would crush the night zenith). Exposure before the bloom threshold. Bloom pyramid resolution-normalised (identical glow at 1080p and 4K). Dither: monochrome TPDF, faded within 0.5 LSB of 0 and 255, dark gain blended over D 0.45 to 0.55. Vignette circular in pixels in half-height units (Hor+).
- **D-015 Far-thread haze toward the local sky colour.** The far thread is mixed toward the sky colour of its own direction (not the horizon colour), so its contrast is exactly (1 - haze) >= 26 % of the unfogged contrast at every elevation (the reason for the 0.74 cap); toward the warm horizon colour the hazed line would cross the sky luminance near 32 degrees in KF1 and vanish.
- **D-016 Sea fog ray length.** For sea/Earth rays the deck term uses the crossing of the planar deck top, not the curved-shell hit distance (otherwise the sea beyond ~15 km would be painted CLOUD_TOP). Non-hit rays below the horizon get the same limit.
- **D-017 Visible sea edge at the Earth limb.** Hits are tested against the cloud-shell limb, but haze is forced to 1 above the R-based limb (dip of 14.7.5) with a 0.35-degree ramp, so the visible edge sits at 2.77 / 5.09 / 23.95 degrees as the acceptances require. Earth haze uses the path inside an 8.5 km layer (`exp(-t/80 km)` literally would hide the Earth at 600 km). The limb glow is gated by `smoothstep(20, 80 km, A)`. The sea fades in over the first 300 m above the cloud tops (~2 s) from the deck white, so emergence does not pop. The Earth drift (0.02 deg/s) rotates the continents only; clouds keep the 0.4 m/s scroll. Stars are extinguished near the horizon while in air; the glint happens only in beat 7.
- **D-018 Collars that meet beads are omitted** (y = 100 lies inside bead S2, y = 200 pierces bead S5's upper surface by 1.4 m). Collars have major radius 50.9 / tube 1.4 (inner 49.5, embedded 0.5 m in the smooth r = 50 cylinder even when the relief fades; the printed 52.5 would float 1.1 m off it).
- **D-019 Cable LOD2 has 54 sides** (spec 64): 54 divides LOD1's 108 vertices per ring, so the stitch is crack-free.
- **D-020 Walkway details.** The deck sweep starts at s = 397 (at s = 396-396.3 the eased deck top is within 0.1 mm of the terrace top and would z-fight); arm roots end at r = 49.7 inside the smooth cylinder (the 51.2 crest of the spec would float up to 0.9 m once the relief fades) or inside a bead lens; bead lenses are cut at r = 48 inside the cable; door S1 at s = 519.5 (clears the arm at s = 521); pad thickness via an underside boss; rail height measured to the top of the handrail tube. The 14-degree grade crease at s = 840 (helix 0.25 to flat pier) is kept: no C1 blend can keep both y(840) = 146 and the specified helix (the slope integral would be short by 1 m).
- **D-021 Audio.** Trapezoidal (Simper) state-variable filters instead of the Chamberlin form (diverges at 14 kHz at 48 kHz); "momentary peak never above -9 dBFS" tested as the sample peak outside the swell window; every "decay" of 14.3.5 read as T60; on the last 20 m "pad only" also fades the drone, hum and stars over s 838-846 and dries the reverb; pad voices enter at s = 150, 240, 330 and 600.5; beat-7 RMS measures -37.7 against -36 (the table rows sum to about -39 in the core of beat 7; within +-3 dB).
- **D-022 Banding metric details (Tools/qa).** The constant-run rule is gated by the local slope (>= 0.06 code/px over 64 px) so a dithered sky that flattens toward the zenith does not falsely fail; a residual gate (RMS <= 0.10, peak-to-peak <= 0.60 code against a 129 px Savitzky-Golay fit) catches undithered gradients that pass the literal metric; per-beat luma gates use display luma.

- **D-023 Bead column shifted up one slot (y_k = 98 + 32 (k - 1) instead of 66 + 32 (k - 1)).** With S1 only 30 m above the terrace, every bead overhang (radius 62 vs cable 50) stacked into a "pagoda" that capped the beat-4 reveal: in KF4a/KF4b the cable ended under a mint lens 30 m overhead, contradicting the money shot's own text ("rising without end ... the lay lines stay visible up to the top edge"). One slot up, KF4a shows the bare ribbed cable past the top edge and KF4b shows S1's rim only at the top edge (foreshadowing kept). The deck still passes bead equators at s = 648 (S1, door) and s = 776 (S2, door; KF7c's bead), so the spec's beat-6 and beat-7 bead moments are unchanged; the s = 520 bead moment (KF5b) becomes an arm-and-cable-wall shot. Doors only where the deck passes the equator. Money shots outrank breadth (Part 1.5).
- **D-024 Deck optical depth in top-relative clamped coordinates.** Above the slab, `F(y1) - F(y0)` subtracted two ~1e7 m values that are mathematically equal; the float rounding (divided by a small `dir.y`) produced per-pixel noise of +-10 codes in every night-sky frame and a speckled line at the horizon. The antiderivative is constant outside the slab, so both ends are clamped into it before evaluation (exact, no cancellation). The C# mirror does the same.
- **D-025 Bank lateral profile `exp(-((x - x_c)/220)^4)`** (flat-topped) instead of `exp(-((x - x_c)/90)^2)`. With the spec profile the terrace corner (x = 190, specified) stood out of the mist as a grey box on the horizon from s = 0, and the bank read as a narrow column rather than the "pale mist bank on the horizon" the spec describes. Width law unchanged (1.95 px at 1080p at s = 0 on the reference geometry; the minimum-width clamp keeps every row >= 1.75 px).

The whole NearRoot (and the instanced posts/windows) is switched on at s = 262 inside the bank, with 4 m hysteresis, like the near cable (14.6).
